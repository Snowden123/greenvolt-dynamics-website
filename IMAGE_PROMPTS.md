# GreenVolt Dynamics: AI image prompts

Save each image into `images/scenes/` with the exact filename below. Export as JPG, quality 80–85, and keep each file under about 500 KB. The page already points at these paths. Until a file exists, the section shows a soft green fallback.

**Shared style line** (append to every prompt):
> Photorealistic documentary photography, natural colour grading, soft morning light, shot on a full-frame camera, shallow haze in the distance, no text, no logos, no watermark, no people looking at camera.

**Shared negative prompt:** text, captions, watermark, logos, cartoon, illustration, oversaturated, HDR glow, distorted hands, extra limbs, Western farm machinery, snow.

## Tips

- Generate at least 2 variations of image 1 and pick the one with the softest, palest sky.
- If a generated image has AI-looking hands or faces, regenerate or crop the people out. Fields and grass are the safest subjects.
- Label any AI-generated images as illustrative if you use them in investor or government material.
